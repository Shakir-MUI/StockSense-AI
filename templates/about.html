ABOUT THE PROJECT

StockSense AI

StockSense AI is a Python-based intelligent stock market analysis and prediction platform designed to analyse Indian stock market data and estimate the possible price direction of stocks for the next trading session.

The application combines data analysis, technical indicators and machine learning techniques to help users understand market movements. It analyses historical stock prices, trading volume and market trends to generate easy-to-understand BUY, SELL or HOLD signals.

Our Project Objective

The main objective of StockSense AI is to make stock market analysis easier and more accessible through a simple and user-friendly web application.

Instead of manually analysing multiple technical indicators, users can select an Indian stock symbol and view its historical performance, technical analysis, predicted price direction and generated trading signal in one place.

How StockSense AI Works

StockSense AI follows a systematic process to analyse stock market information and generate predictions.

  • Data Collection: Historical daily stock market data is collected for the selected NSE-listed stock.
  • Data Processing: Python and Pandas are used to clean, organise and prepare the historical data for analysis.
  • Technical Analysis: Indicators such as RSI, SMA, EMA, daily returns and volume changes are calculated to identify market trends.
  • Machine Learning: A Random Forest classifier analyses historical patterns and estimates the probability of an upward or downward price movement in the next trading session.
  • Signal Generation: The model prediction and technical indicator conditions are combined to generate a BUY, SELL or HOLD signal.

Key Features

  • Analysis of supported Indian stock market symbols.
  • Historical stock price and volume analysis.
  • Technical indicators including RSI, SMA and EMA.
  • Machine learning-based price direction prediction.
  • BUY, SELL and HOLD signal generation.
  • Prediction probability and confidence information.
  • Prediction history for reviewing previous analyses.
  • User registration and secure login functionality.
  • Interactive charts for understanding stock performance.

Prediction Reliability Engine

One of the important components of StockSense AI is its Prediction Reliability Engine. It provides additional context for interpreting model predictions by considering factors such as historical model performance, prediction confidence and market conditions.

This feature helps users understand that not every prediction has the same level of reliability. It encourages users to evaluate prediction results carefully rather than depending only on a BUY or SELL signal.

Technology Stack

  • Programming Language: Python
  • Web Framework: Flask
  • Data Analysis: Pandas and NumPy
  • Machine Learning: Scikit-learn
  • Data Visualisation: Matplotlib / Plotly
  • Database: SQLite
  • Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Bootstrap

Important Limitations

StockSense AI is an analytical project. Stock market prices are influenced by several unpredictable factors, including economic conditions, company announcements, market sentiment and global events.

Market data may be delayed, incomplete or unavailable. Machine learning predictions, confidence scores and historical performance do not guarantee future results.

A SELL signal is intended as a warning for existing shareholders to review their investment strategy.
