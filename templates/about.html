ABOUT THE PROJECT

StockSense AI

A Python-based application for analysing Indian stock data and estimating next-session price direction.

How the signal is created

  • Historical daily stock data is downloaded for the selected NSE symbol.
  • Python calculates RSI, moving averages, daily returns and volume changes.
  • A Random Forest classifier estimates the probability of an upward or downward next-session move.
  • Simple indicator checks are used to decide whether the application displays BUY, SELL or HOLD.

Important limitations

The market data may be delayed, incomplete or unavailable. Model probabilities and historical accuracy do not guarantee future results. A SELL signal is a warning for someone holding a stock to review their own plan; it is not a short-selling instruction.
